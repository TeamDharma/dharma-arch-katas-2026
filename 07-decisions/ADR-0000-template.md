# ADR-NNNN: Short noun phrase

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN

## Context

What problem are we solving, and what did we know when we decided? State the
forces honestly, including the ones that pull against the decision we made.
List the alternatives we actually considered, not a strawman set.

## Decision

What we chose, stated plainly, and why. The "why" is the part a reader cannot
reconstruct from the code or the diagrams, so it carries the weight.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it commits us to. Mark
the bad news with a minus sign. An ADR whose consequences are all positive is
not finished.

## Verification

How we will know whether this decision is working once it is live. For any
decision involving a model, name the metric, the threshold, and what happens
automatically when the threshold is breached.
